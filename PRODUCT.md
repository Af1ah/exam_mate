# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Competitive-exam aspirants studying primarily on mobile. The MVP begins with SSC preparation but must support additional exam categories as the question catalog grows.

## Product Purpose

Exam Mate gives learners one focused, ten-question practice set each day. It makes consistent exam preparation feel manageable and lets learners revisit the quizzes they completed on earlier days.

## Positioning

Exam Mate is a private, WhatsApp-first daily practice companion: it delivers one fresh topic at a time, preserves completed day-wise attempts in a calendar, and avoids repeating questions a learner has already seen.

## Operating Context

Learners receive a private quiz link through WhatsApp, complete a timed ten-question set, review their answers, and return to the dashboard to see past attendance and scores by quiz day.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- MVP: one 10-question quiz per day, with fresh questions each day.
- The calendar must make previous-day completed quizzes visible and accessible.
- The question catalog is maintained through CSV imports. The current import schema is represented by `ssc_cgl_questions.csv`.
- Question content starts with SSC but is not limited to SSC.

## Brand Commitments

Exam Mate is an encouraging exam companion. Its language should be calm, specific, and supportive of steady preparation—not overly celebratory, generic, or distracting.

## Evidence on Hand

- A 300-question source catalog: `/home/aflah/Downloads/ssc_cgl_questions.csv`.
- The application’s existing quiz, results, dashboard, WhatsApp link, and database schema.
- No approved logo system, testimonials, outcome claims, or visual brand assets are currently recorded.

## Product Principles

1. Reward showing up and sustained practice, not only high scores.
2. Keep the daily study task focused enough to fit a real routine.
3. Make each learner’s history useful for deciding what to practise next.
4. Keep the experience ready for more exam categories without changing the core habit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The mobile web experience must remain legible, touch-friendly, keyboard-accessible, and usable at narrow viewport widths.
